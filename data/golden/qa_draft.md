# Draft Q&A pairs (to be reviewed before use in the golden set)

 ## GL-01
**Q1.** What is the standard starting dose of Cardiolan, and how often is it taken?
**A1.** 5 mg once daily, in the morning (Clause 4.1).

**Q2.** What is the maximum Cardiolan dose for a patient with an eGFR of 45 mL/min/1.73 m²?
**A2.** 10 mg once daily (Clause 6.3).

**Q3.** By what percentage of rise in serum creatinine within 2 weeks of a dose change must Cardiolan be reduced to the previous dose?
**A3.** More than 30% from baseline (Clause 6.5).

**Q4.** What is the maximum combined Cardiolan dose when a patient is taking Renaprotect at 40 mg or more per day?
**A4.** 10 mg once daily (Clause 7.1).

**Q5.** What resting heart rate at any review requires a one-step dose reduction and an electrocardiogram?
**A5.** A resting heart rate below 50 beats per minute (Clause 8.5).

## GL-02

**Q1.** What is the recommended starting dose of Glucorex, and with which meal is it taken?
**A1.** 500 mg once daily with the evening meal (Clause 4.1).

**Q2.** What is the maximum total daily dose of Glucorex for a patient with an eGFR of 40 mL/min/1.73 m²?
**A2.** 1000 mg per day (Clause 6.4).

**Q3.** At what eGFR must Glucorex be stopped immediately?
**A3.** Below 30 mL/min/1.73 m² (Clause 6.5).

**Q4.** For how long after an iodinated contrast procedure must Glucorex be withheld?
**A4.** On the day of the procedure and for 48 hours afterwards (Clause 6.6).

**Q5.** How soon should fasting glucose be reviewed in a patient using Pulmavent more than 4 times per day?
**A5.** Within 2 weeks (Clause 7.4).


## GL-03-v1 (v1.0, superseded)
**Q1.** What is the Antibiox dose and duration for mild pneumonia?
**A1.** 500 mg orally three times daily, every 8 hours, for 5 days (Clause 4.1).

**Q2.** What is the maximum total daily dose of Antibiox?
**A2.** 3000 mg per day (Clause 4.4).

**Q3.** What is the Antibiox regimen for a patient with an eGFR of 45 mL/min/1.73 m²?
**A3.** The interval is extended to every 12 hours, with a maximum of 1000 mg twice daily (Clause 6.3).

**Q4.** After how many hours of treatment without improvement is clinical failure defined?
**A4.** After 72 hours (Clause 8.3).

**Q5.** At what point after starting Antibiox must creatinine and potassium be checked in a patient taking Renaprotect?
**A5.** At 48 hours (Clause 7.4).

## GL-03 (v2.0, current)
**Q1.** What is the Antibiox dose and duration for mild pneumonia in this version?
**A1.** 250 mg orally four times daily, every 6 hours, for 7 days (Clause 4.1).

**Q2.** What is the maximum total daily dose of Antibiox?
**A2.** 2000 mg per day (Clause 4.4).

**Q3.** After how many hours of treatment without improvement is clinical failure defined?
**A3.** After 96 hours (Clause 8.3).

**Q4.** How long after normal eating and drinking are re-established should Glucorex be restarted following acute illness?
**A4.** 72 hours (Clause 7.2).

**Q5.** At what CRB score must a patient be admitted urgently, and what is the total Antibiox duration for that group?
**A5.** A CRB score of 2 or higher; 14 days in total (Clauses 3.4 and 4.3).

## GL-04
**Q1.** What is the maximum dose of Pulmavent in 24 hours outside an exacerbation?
**A1.** 8 puffs (800 micrograms) (Clause 4.4).

**Q2.** What is the Pulmavent dose for a mild to moderate exacerbation, and how long may it be repeated?
**A2.** 4 puffs via a spacer, repeated every 20 minutes for up to 1 hour (Clause 4.5).

**Q3.** When should Pulmavent response be reviewed after Cardiolan is started?
**A3.** 2 weeks after Cardiolan is started (Clause 7.1).

**Q4.** For a patient on Glucorex, how many Pulmavent uses per day should trigger a fasting glucose review within 2 weeks?
**A4.** More than 4 times per day (Clause 7.2).

**Q5.** What PEF range defines a severe acute exacerbation?
**A5.** 33% to 49% of personal best or predicted (Clause 2.4).
